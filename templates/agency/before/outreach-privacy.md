# How {{AGENCY_NAME}} handles your business details

Last updated {{DATE}}.

## What we collect

Only business contact details a business has published itself: business name, public phone and
email, address, website and social pages, and where we found them. We do not buy lists and we do not
collect personal details about owners or staff.

## Why we contact you

We write to businesses about their own website, using the contact details they published for
business enquiries. Every message says who we are, gives our mailing address, and has a one-step way
to stop further messages.

## Stopping messages

Reply "stop" or "remove", or email {{AGENCY_EMAIL}}. We stop all messages within 10 business days
(usually the same day) and delete your details, keeping only your email or phone on a do-not-contact
list so we never contact you by mistake.

## Who sees it

Only {{AGENCY_NAME}}. We do not sell or share your details. They are stored on our own computer and
the email service we use to send messages.

## How long we keep it

If you don't become a client, we delete your details within 90 days of our last message.
