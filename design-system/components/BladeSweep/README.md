Six thin shards raking across the middle of the frame at a tilt while a tool runs, with the tool's name riding in on the sweep.

Use it for the whole of a tool call and nothing else. It plays in the band from 14% to 30% of the frame so the header and the transcript stay legible, masked to fade at both ends and both sides.

The consumer provides the tool name. Everything is transform and opacity on the compositor; nothing here touches layout, which matters over a live 3D scene.

- A `blade` is a vertical gradient (accent mixed 70% with white at its brightest) with a 22px accent glow, skewed between 8° and 27°, sweeping from -18vw to 116vw on `ease-blade`.
- The six blades differ in width (1–16px), tilt, tempo (1.15–2.6s) and delay, deliberately co-prime, so the pattern never visibly repeats within a tool call. The wide dim ones are the glow the sharp ones travel in.
- `blade-tool` is `mono`, 10px, 0.3em, uppercase, `ink-blade` with `glow-blade-tool`, prefixed ⟩, crossing on `t-blade-carry` with `ease-carry`: it rides in, holds nearly still long enough to read, then leaves.
- Reduced motion: the blades park where they would have crossed at 55% of their opacity, and the name sits centred.
